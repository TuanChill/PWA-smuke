# Plan: Swipe News Curation & AI Social Post Drafting (Option A)

## Overview
Implement Option A: Seamless in-feed news curation and social post generation. Users swipe left to skip irrelevant news, swipe right to like/curate relevant stories, and tap the Comment button on the floating action dock to open an In-Deck Bottom Sheet Drawer. There, they can enter their quick perspective, select a target social platform (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Executive Memo), and let AI draft a high-impact, contextualized social post with the source article link.
